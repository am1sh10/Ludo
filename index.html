How this game is played

  • Everyone rolls at the start. The first 6 goes first, and that 6 counts as a real move.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
  • Roll again after a 6, a capture, or getting a token home.
  • Three 6s in a row lose your turn.
  • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to its base. If two are stacked, both go back.
  • Squares with a star, and each color's start square, are safe. Tokens share them.
  • Tokens of the same color can stack, and they never block anyone.
  • You need the exact roll to reach the center.
  • The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.
